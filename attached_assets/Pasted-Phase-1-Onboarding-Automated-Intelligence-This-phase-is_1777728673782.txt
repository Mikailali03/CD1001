Phase 1: Onboarding & Automated Intelligence
This phase is designed to provide "Instant Value" by reducing data entry friction through third-party integrations.
1. Smart Property Initialization
Workflow: User enters a street address. The app triggers a fetch to the RentCast API, which returns the Year Built, Square Footage, Bedrooms, Bathrooms, and Lot Size. The user verifies/edits this data before committing.
The AI Scout Hook: Upon commitment, the app automatically triggers the "Property Scout" Edge Function. This AI researches the local climate and home age to generate a "Whole-Home Stewardship Plan" (e.g., Gutter cleaning, winterizing hose bibs).
Interconnectivity: These tasks are automatically assigned to a default "Home Stewardship" asset, ensuring that general home care has a historical log just like a physical appliance.
Phase 2: Asset Management & The "AI Lens"
The core of the dossier is the catalog of physical systems (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical, etc.).
2. AI Lens Intake (Multimodal OCR)
Workflow: User clicks "Add Asset" and selects "Use Camera" or "Browse Files." A photo of the technical specification plate is sent as a Base64 string to the Gemini 2.5 Flash Edge Function.
Extraction: The AI extracts the Brand, Model Number, Serial Number, Manufacture Date, and Estimated Replacement Cost, then auto-fills the modal fields.
Interconnectivity: This accuracy directly powers the Capital Outlook (math depends on the date) and the Technician Handoff (techs need serial numbers).
3. Autonomous Task Generation
Workflow: Once an asset is saved, the AI Research Agent identifies the official manufacturer-recommended maintenance schedule for that specific brand/model.
Detailed Content: It generates not just a title (e.g., "Replace Filter"), but a detailed, bulleted "How Do I...?" instruction set.
Interconnectivity: These tasks feed the Maintenance Inbox and the Home Health Score.
Phase 3: The Maintenance Operating System (OS)
This is the "Living" part of the app where the homeowner performs actions.
4. Maintenance Inbox (The Carousel)
Workflow: A dashboard carousel that filters and sorts all tasks (Hardware + Stewardship) due within the next 30 days.
Prioritization: Items are sorted chronologically (Soonest first). Clicking the arrow takes the user directly to that asset's deep-dive record.
5. Unified Service Logging (The "Chain of Custody")
Workflow: Clicking "Complete" on a task or "Log Service" opens a unified modal. The user toggles between DIY (Self-Performed) or Professional.
Validation: For Professional service, users select a pro from their Directory, enter the cost, and attach an image/PDF of the invoice.
Date Recalculation: Upon saving, the system calculates the next due date by adding the frequency_months to the actual date performed, resetting the maintenance clock perfectly.
Interconnectivity: Every dollar entered here is instantly aggregated in the Investment Ledger.
Phase 4: Financial & Capital Planning
This phase addresses the "Home as an Investment" persona.
6. Capital Outlook (10-Year Liability Horizon)
Workflow: Uses the Manufacture Date and Industry Longevity Standards to project the "Death Date" of every major system.
Visualization: An interactive Recharts Line Graph showing projected spend over the next decade. Hovering over data points reveals which specific assets are due for replacement and their estimated cost.
7. Investment Ledger (The Cost Basis)
Workflow: A unified financial ledger that merges Capital Expenses (Original Install Costs) and Operating Expenses (Service/Maintenance Logs).
Visualization: A Pie Chart showing "Capital Allocation" by category (e.g., 60% of spend is in HVAC) and a chronological transaction audit.
Phase 5: Documentation & The Physical Bridge
The final phase makes the data portable and physically accessible.
8. QR Identity Protocol (The Tech Bridge)
Workflow: Every asset (except Stewardship) generates a unique CDA Protocol QR Code (CDA|UUID|Registry_ID).
Physical Tag: Homeowners can print these labels to stick on their actual equipment.
Interconnectivity: When scanned by the Technician App, it bypasses the search UI and lands the tech directly on that specific unit's history and manual.
9. Verified Report Generation
Workflow: A one-tap button generates a high-contrast, black-and-white, Print-Optimized Audit.
Content: It includes property specs, active system inventory, and a Decommissioned Systems Archive (soft-retired assets). Costs are hidden to maintain homeowner privacy while proving technical care.
10. Digital Deed Transfer
Workflow: Homeowner enters a recipient's email in Settings.
Escrow Logic: If the user exists, a Postgres RPC Function performs an instant ownership handshake. If they don't exist, an invite is created in "Escrow," and a Resend Webhook emails the new owner to claim the history.
Interconnectivity: Ensures the "Carfax" narrative stays with the house, not the person.
